Slow is smooth.

Tap Start or hold Forward, then hold ↑ to accelerate. Use ← and → to steer. Hold ↓ or Space to brake.

Stop before changing gear. Press G or select D / R. In reverse, keep using the gas pedal: steering direction acts on the front wheels, so the rear swings the other way.

Choose Top for a close overhead follow camera, or Driver for a view through the windscreen with live left and right side mirrors. Look back switches the driver camera behind you; selecting Reverse also shows the rear view. The mobile game uses a landscape layout. Rotate the bottom-left steering wheel by tracing a circle around its rim; in portrait, the game rotates sideways, so turn your phone for comfortable driving. Hold Forward or Reverse at the bottom right to move. Releasing Forward applies smooth automatic braking; use Brake to stop firmly. Changing direction brakes before switching gear. Release the thumb to centre the steering. The green bay is your destination; stop with your entire car inside it.

Test mode ends on a cone hit or track exit. The L-track allows one forward adjustment during the return. Its 3-minute timer is a simulator challenge setting, not an official time limit.
